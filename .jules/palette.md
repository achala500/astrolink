## 2026-11-08 - Dark-Sky Scotopic Vision Preservation with Theme-Adaptive Focus Indicators
**Learning:** In astrophotography applications where observers use red/crimson night-vision modes to preserve dark adaptation (scotopic vision), standard blue or emerald keyboard focus rings can ruin rhodopsin eye adaptation or cause screen flash. Interactive controls must adapt focus indicator hue and offset based on the active night vision theme (`focus-visible:ring-red-500` in OLED Crimson mode versus `focus-visible:ring-emerald-400` in standard dark mode) alongside descriptive `aria-pressed` and `aria-label` attributes for icon-only buttons.
**Action:** When adding keyboard navigation and focus rings to control bars or modals, ensure focus styles dynamically bind to `isCrimson` and include explicit `aria-label` and `aria-pressed` state attributes.
